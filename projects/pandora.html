About the project




I was hired by OPP Studio to work as a UI Designer for Legacy of Pandora. A monster taming game where you play as Pyrrha, using monsters named Kakodemons to fight. These Kakodemons are caught using a lamp and stored into a Jar.

My goal was to use the jar and incorporate it into the UI creating an immersive yet simple to use experience. The idea was to use the jar and show kakodemons on the side of the jar's body. The jar would present one demon at a time. You could select/cycle through your availalbe demons by rotating the Jar horizontally.

We had 5 different tabs:
- SUMMARY
- MOVESET
- INVEST
- CORRUPT


To integrate these tabs in the same immersive jar twisting selection, i moved them to be displayed one at a time on the jar's neck.

Initially, the 5 monster slots that represent your PARTY, were displayed vertically on left hand side. But cycling through slots vertically while the jar content rotates vertically had a broken flow.

So i decided to list the slots at the bottom. This however didn't leave as much space as we wanted for navigation inputs. Since the top tabs were placed on the Jar's neck now, we freed up space uptop for navigation (which is usually at the bottom). This made all things fall into place with a satisfying "click" resulting in the diagram below.



Monster Management - Main Screens

Another nice feature of this flow is that player can cycle through the party monster slots while having a tab selected. So for example, you can switch to the MOVESET tab then cycle your party monsters to quickly check the moves of each monster slot.







Jar's Neck & Body




So the end result was a jar that can change its body content by:
1. Rotating the jar body to cycle monsters
2. Twist the jar neck to cycle tabs. Each tab chaging the type of content we display on the jar's body.


Monster Management Screens



Bestiary

I had to make a Bestiary screen where player can learn more about each demon collected and their lore. I put myself into Pyrrha's shoes thinking how would a character keep details about these creatures. The answer the obviously a book. I used black pages to convey the idea of darkness, forbidden and power.

Battle End Screen


The victory and defeat icons are based on wheat, a symbol of growth, abundance, and prosperity in Greek culture. Victory is represented by healthy, upright wheat to convey progression, reward, and flourishing. Defeat is shown through fallen, dried wheat, as well a lack of color symbolizing loss.
