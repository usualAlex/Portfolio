About the game

A stylized 2D realistic physics driving game set in a futuristic city outskirts about scrap cars driven off a cliff.
Goal is to reach the bottom finish line.


My initial intent was to used voronoi based destruction physics or joint-based pre-broken parts. Currently it's unclear how I want to approach this.




Test car based on Chilli - Flatout 2


So far:

- Made a test car inpired by Flatout 2's Chili car (my favorite car in the game).
- Made a custom menu navigation for m&k and controllers. The default Unity behavior was very limiting.
- Drivable cars with little parts flying off.
- Garage upgrade system that allows players to re-configure data points for their cars.
- Highly tuned physics and vehicle control for a 2d car game.
